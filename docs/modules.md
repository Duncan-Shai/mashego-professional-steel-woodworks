# System Modules

## 1. Customer Management
Stores customer records.

## 2. Enquiries / Leads
Tracks new customer requests.

## 3. Measurements
Stores dimensions and site details.

## 4. Quotations
Handles estimates and approvals.

## 5. Job Cards
Tracks approved work.

## 6. Workshop Management
Tracks production tasks.

## 7. Inventory
Tracks steel, timber, and materials.

## 8. Quality Control
Checks work before delivery.

## 9. Installation
Tracks site installation.

## 10. Invoicing
Handles billing and payments.

## 11. Reports
Shows business performance.

## 12. Support / Warranty
Handles after-sales issues.
