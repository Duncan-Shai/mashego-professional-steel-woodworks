# Business Workflow

## Step 1: Customer Enquiry
The customer submits a request.

## Step 2: Capture Details
Staff records customer details, project type, and description.

## Step 3: Site Measurement
If needed, the team measures the site.

## Step 4: Quote Creation
A quotation is created and sent to the customer.

## Step 5: Customer Approval
The customer approves the quotation.

## Step 6: Job Card Creation
A job card is created for workshop production.

## Step 7: Workshop Production
Steel or woodwork is fabricated.

## Step 8: Quality Control
The finished work is checked.

## Step 9: Installation
The work is installed on site.

## Step 10: Invoice and Closeout
The job is completed and invoiced.
